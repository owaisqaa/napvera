The brands we work with.

Over three decades our team has imported and distributed food and beverage brands across Syria, Türkiye and Europe. Here are the brands we carry today and have carried along the way, starting with our own.

Our own brand

Levant

Roasted seeds

Levant is our own label, carrying on the private-label work our team has done since before 2011. It brings the Levant's favourite snack to the shelf in retail packs: sunflower, Egyptian, black, red, zucchini and pumpkin seeds.

Brands we carry and have carried

  • Al Ahlam

    Damascus countryside, Syria · Since 1974

    A family-owned Syrian food producer founded in 1974. Table olives, olive oil, preserved vegetables, grape leaves, molasses and pantry essentials.

  • Hamwi Café

    Damascus, Syria · Since 1951

    Founded in Damascus in 1951, when Mohammad Abd Al Wahab Al Hamwi opened his first shop grinding fresh coffee. The family later built its own coffee factory and expanded to the UAE.

  • Alsuhoul

    By Sweid · Since 1963

    Traditional red and green Aleppo thyme blends, the za'atar of Syrian breakfast tables.

  • Hana

    Canned food and pantry staples

    Canned meat and fish, pulses, mezze, pickles and pantry staples, from single tins to catering sizes.

  • Katakit

    Biscuits and wafers

    Cream-filled, digestive and coated biscuits and wafers in assorted flavours and pack sizes.

  • Squeeze

    A Katakit brand

    Instant powdered fruit drinks in single-serve sachets and a range of flavours.

  • Cherry Brand

    Ceylon tea, Sri Lanka

    Ceylon black tea, loose leaf and tea bags, in classic and flavoured varieties.
