Built in Syria, proven abroad, back home.

The same people, the same trade, three different markets. Here is the road that led to Napvera.

  1. Before 2011

    Syria

    Our founders imported and distributed food and beverages across Syria, built their own private labels, and brought in products from major Turkish brands.

  2. Türkiye

    Mumlar

    When the work moved abroad, the team carried on trading in Türkiye through Mumlar, staying close to the manufacturers they had worked with for years.

  3. Europe

    Swenap

    Through Swenap in the Netherlands, the team brought Middle Eastern food products to retailers in the Netherlands, Germany, France, Belgium, Sweden and Austria.

  4. Today

    Napvera, Damascus

    Now we are bringing that experience home. From Maisat Square we supply Syrian wholesalers and retailers with the brands and the service they can count on.

What we do

We handle the whole path a product takes into Syria, from the supplier's door to the shop shelf.

What we carry

  • Tea and coffee
  • Oils and ghee
  • Canned food
  • Rice, grains and pulses
  • Snacks and sweets
  • Beverages

Import

We source food and beverage products from Türkiye, Europe and beyond, and take care of shipping, customs and paperwork to bring them into Syria.

Distribution

We supply wholesalers, supermarkets and shops, keeping stock moving so products reach the shelf on time.

Brand representation

Brands entering Syria get a local partner who knows the market, the trade and the people in it.

Private label

We develop products under our own labels, as we did for years before 2011.
